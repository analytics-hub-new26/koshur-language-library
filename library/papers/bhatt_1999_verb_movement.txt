1. THE V2 PARADOX IN INDO-ARYAN
The standard Indo-Aryan clause structure has long been characterized as head-final with canonical SOV order and right-headed functional projections (IP and VP). Kashmiri challenges this pan-South Asian generalization by exhibiting left-headed CP with obligatory movement of the finite verb to C0.

2. DERIVATION OF THE ROOT CLAUSE
Under Bhatt's generative framework:
- The finite verb undergoes V-to-I movement to collect tense and inflectional features.
- In root clauses, the finite verb moves from I0 to C0.
- A maximal projection (XP) topicalizes or moves to Spec,CP to satisfy the V2 criterion (EPP on C).
    [CP XP [C' V+I_i [IP ... t_i ... [VP ... ]]]]

3. WH-MOVEMENT & INTERROGATIVE CONSTRUCTIONS
Wh-phrases in Kashmiri root interrogatives move obligatorily to the position immediately preceding the finite verb (Spec,CP):
    kemy por yi akhbaar?
    who-ERG read-PST this newspaper
    'Who read this newspaper?'
If an adverbial is fronted ahead of the wh-word, the wh-word remains immediately pre-verbal, demonstrating that Kashmiri wh-elements target the designated specifier of the functional projection hosting the finite verb.

4. IMPLICATIONS FOR UNIVERSAL GRAMMAR
Kashmiri provides crucial empirical evidence that V2 is not an exclusively Germanic property, and that language contact and areal diffusion can reshape syntactic macro-parameters in Indo-Aryan.
