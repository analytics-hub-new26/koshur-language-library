1. DISCOURSE PARTICLES & SARCASM
In conversational Kashmiri, irony and sarcastic negation are grammaticalized through a dedicated construction involving the enclitic particle '-ay' and intonational pitch accent resetting:
    su chhu-ay bod daanavan!
    he is-SARC big genius
    'As if he were a great genius!'

2. PRAGMATIC CONSTRAINTS
Unlike overt negation ('na' / 'ne'), the sarcasm construction asserts the affirmative proposition with inverted pragmatic polarity, universally recognized by native speakers across both Srinagar and rural speech communities.
